## Round note

**Round 1** explored four directions — *Warm Companion*, *Calm Keeper*, *Gentle Coach*, and
*Living Garden*. Of these, **Gentle Coach** resonated most and is **carried forward** as the
anchor; the other three were set aside (their useful idea — *Calm Keeper's* explicit
local-first/privacy framing — remains available to graft onto any winner). See git history for
the round-1 versions.

**Round 2 (this doc)** keeps Gentle Coach and adds **three genuinely new territories** —
*Orbit*, *Pulse*, and *Thread* — deliberately distinct from each other and from the round-1
ideas, to widen the search for a brand the owner actually loves.

**The health rings are now open.** The existing green/yellow/red ring around each avatar is
no longer treated as fixed. Each direction below proposes its *own* status indicator, and the
chosen brand can redefine that visual entirely.

### How to read this

Each profile is a complete territory — personality, voice, visual feel, a positioning line,
its own **status indicator** (replacing the health ring), and the same three **in-voice
touchpoints** so they compare apples-to-apples:

1. **Daily nudge** — the local notification that opens the ritual (keystone of Direction A).
2. **Deck-cleared empty state** — the reward moment.
3. **Weekly recap** — the positive reinforcement beat.

The name **"Kin"** is fixed; these directions express it differently.

## Profile 1 — "The Gentle Coach" *(carried forward from Round 1)*

**The idea:** Kin makes staying in touch feel good — a small, satisfying daily win. It leans
into "delight as a game," but *gently*: encouragement and streaks, never pressure or shame.
The energy of a supportive coach, not a drill sergeant.

- **Name treatment / wordmark:** "Kin" in a friendly, rounded, bolder geometric; bright and
  optimistic. Room for a small, warm character/mascot or an expressive thread-knot mark that
  can animate (celebrate on a deck-clear).
- **Personality:** Upbeat, encouraging, a touch playful. Celebrates effort. Has a smile.
- **Voice & tone:** Friendly and motivating, light humor, tasteful emoji. Celebratory on wins,
  *forgiving* on misses — "welcome back," never "you broke your streak." Reframes the chore as a win.
- **Visual feel:**
  - *Color:* brighter, cheerful palette — a confident primary (coral/blue), playful secondaries.
  - *Type:* rounded geometric sans, friendly and bold.
  - *Icon/illustration:* expressive, characterful illustrations; motion, confetti (already in the
    app on deck-clear), satisfying micro-animations.
  - **Status indicator — "connection meter":** a playful progress ring you keep topped up; gentle
    pulse when due. Green = thriving; the overdue state nudges, it doesn't scold.
- **Positioning:** *For people who want a friendlier social life with less guilt — Kin turns
  staying in touch into a small daily win you'll actually look forward to.*
- **Touchpoints (in voice):**
  - **Daily nudge:** *"Ready for today's 3? 🎯 A few good people are waiting to hear from you."*
  - **Deck cleared:** *"Deck cleared! 🎉 12-day streak of being a great friend. See you tomorrow."*
  - **Weekly recap:** *"What a week — 8 people, 11 chats! 🌟 You're on a roll. (And hey, if next week's quieter, that's okay too.)"*

## Profile 2 — "Orbit"

**The idea:** The people you love are a small universe that orbits you. Some are close and
bright; others slowly drift toward the dark if you don't keep them in orbit. Kin is the map of
your personal constellation — calm, premium, and quietly awe-inspiring. It reframes "staying in
touch" from a to-do list into *keeping your people close*.

- **Name treatment / wordmark:** "Kin" in a clean, slightly futuristic geometric; a small
  orbiting dot circling a letter, or three constellation points forming a quiet mark. Refined,
  dark-mode-native.
- **Personality:** Calm wonder and perspective. Makes you feel the bigness and preciousness of
  your people without preaching. Spacious, not busy.
- **Voice & tone:** Spare but evocative; cosmic metaphor used with a light hand. Never gushy.
  "Mara's drifting toward the edge." Quietly poetic, grown-up.
- **Visual feel:**
  - *Color:* deep indigo / night-sky base with soft glows and gradients; warm gold star-points as
    accent. A genuinely beautiful dark mode (with a bright "daylight sky" alt).
  - *Type:* refined geometric sans, generous spacing.
  - *Icon/illustration:* starfields, soft orbital paths, gentle glows; restrained, premium.
  - **Status indicator — "brightness & orbit":** each person is a star. In touch = bright and in a
    close orbit; as time passes they *dim* and *drift outward*. The deck is "the stars that need
    tending tonight." Naturally scales to large networks — your whole sky at a glance — which the
    green/yellow/red ring never did.
- **Positioning:** *For people whose world is full of people worth keeping close — Kin maps your
  personal constellation so no one you love drifts quietly into the dark.*
- **Touchpoints (in voice):**
  - **Daily nudge:** *"Three of your people are drifting to the edge tonight. A quick hello pulls them back into orbit."*
  - **Deck cleared:** *"Your sky's bright tonight — everyone's close."*
  - **Weekly recap:** *"This week you brightened 8 connections. Mara's back in a close orbit."*

## Profile 3 — "Pulse"

**The idea:** Every relationship has a rhythm. Kin helps you *keep the beat* — a steady pulse of
small touches that keeps a connection alive. This leans straight into the app's own core
mechanic (`cadence_days`): a relationship is healthy when it has a regular beat, and Kin makes
that beat visible and easy to keep. Modern and energetic without being cozy or childish.

- **Name treatment / wordmark:** "Kin" in a confident contemporary grotesk; a small EKG/waveform
  tick or a beat-dot that can subtly pulse in motion. Sharp, current, alive.
- **Personality:** Energetic, modern, in-the-flow. Warm but contemporary — momentum, not hype.
  The feeling of a life that's actively connected, not maintained out of duty.
- **Voice & tone:** Punchy, present-tense, rhythmic. Motivating without the coach's cheerleading.
  "Keep the beat with your people." Confident and concise.
- **Visual feel:**
  - *Color:* a vivid but sophisticated accent (electric coral or violet) over clean light/dark;
    energetic but not loud.
  - *Type:* tight, modern grotesk.
  - *Icon/illustration:* waveform / pulse motifs, equalizer bars, subtle kinetic motion.
  - **Status indicator — "the beat":** a pulse/waveform around or beneath the avatar. A strong,
    steady beat = healthy; as someone goes quiet the beat *slows and flattens* toward a flatline.
    Reaching out restarts the rhythm. A perfect visual fit for "cadence" — and a lively,
    legible alternative to a static colored ring.
- **Positioning:** *For people who want their closest bonds to stay in rhythm — Kin keeps the
  beat, so the people who matter never go quiet.*
- **Touchpoints (in voice):**
  - **Daily nudge:** *"Three people are slipping out of rhythm. A quick hello keeps the beat going."*
  - **Deck cleared:** *"Everyone's in rhythm today. Nice tempo."*
  - **Weekly recap:** *"8 people, 11 touches — you kept a steady beat this week."*

## Profile 4 — "Thread"

**The idea:** Relationships are threads between people. Left alone they fray; tended, they hold.
Kin is the loom — where you weave new threads and mend the ones that are wearing thin. A
tactile, crafted, design-forward take: care expressed through making. Warm like Round 1's
Companion, but with a maker's sophistication rather than cozy softness. (It also literally names
the "thread" of a conversation.)

- **Name treatment / wordmark:** "Kin" with a woven or single-stroke "thread" feel — a looping
  line, a small knot, or a stitched accent that ties the letters together. Tasteful and designy.
- **Personality:** Thoughtful, intentional, quietly warm. The sensibility of someone who makes
  things by hand and means them.
- **Voice & tone:** Warm and tactile; craft metaphor, lightly. "Your thread with Dad is fraying
  a little." Gentle, considered, never twee.
- **Visual feel:**
  - *Color:* textile palette — warm woven tones, indigo, ecru, one rich accent; subtle
    weave/fabric texture.
  - *Type:* humanist sans with character; could pair with a fine accent face.
  - *Icon/illustration:* line-as-thread illustration, loops, knots, weaves.
  - **Status indicator — "the strand":** a woven thread or loop around the avatar — taut and richly
    colored when the bond is fresh; thinning and *fraying* as it's neglected; re-tightening and
    brightening when you reach out. A strand, not a ring — distinctive and tactile, and the
    "fraying" read communicates *recoverable*, not *failed*.
- **Positioning:** *For people who want to hold onto the bonds that matter — Kin is where you
  weave and mend the threads that connect you to the people you love.*
- **Touchpoints (in voice):**
  - **Daily nudge:** *"Three threads could use a little mending today — Mara, Dad, and Leo."*
  - **Deck cleared:** *"Every thread's holding strong today."*
  - **Weekly recap:** *"This week you mended 8 threads. The one with Mara is good and tight again."*

## Notes for the persona test (kin-5ec.2)

Things to probe when scoring these four against the personas:

- **The guilt line.** Every direction must clear the brief's bar: gentle, forgiving, never
  shaming. Watch for any profile a persona reads as naggy or judgmental (e.g. a "flatlining"
  Pulse or a "drifting into the dark" Orbit could tip ominous if mishandled).
- **The age split (still the key hypothesis).** Does Coach skew young and read juvenile to
  40–55s? Do Orbit / Pulse / Thread read more grown-up while keeping delight?
- **Metaphor legibility across cultures** (AU / EU / US / Latam / Dubai / Singapore) — do orbit,
  heartbeat, and weaving metaphors translate? Note where one travels better than another.
- **The privacy lever.** None of these four foregrounds local-first the way Round 1's Calm Keeper
  did. If privacy is a strong purchase driver for several personas, recommend grafting that
  framing onto the winner.
- **Hybrids welcome.** kin-5ec.2 may recommend a winner *plus* a graft (e.g. "Pulse's rhythm
  system with Coach's warmth and forgiving streaks," or "Orbit's visual world with explicit
  privacy framing").
- **If none land:** Round 3. These four are not the last word — the goal is a brand the owner loves.
