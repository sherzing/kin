## How to read this

This document presents **four distinct brand directions** — deliberately *different*, not
four shades of one idea — so the persona testing in kin-5ec.2 has genuine range. Each is a
complete territory: a personality, a voice, a visual feel, a positioning line, and the same
three **in-voice touchpoints** so they can be compared apples-to-apples:

1. **Daily nudge** — the local notification that opens the ritual (the keystone of Direction A).
2. **Deck-cleared empty state** — the reward moment (today the PRD's "Relax, you're a good friend!").
3. **Weekly recap** — the positive reinforcement beat.

The name **"Kin"** is fixed; these directions express it differently.

A note on the **health rings** (the existing green/yellow/red ring around each avatar): each
profile reinterprets that same mechanic in its own language, which is a good test of how
ownable each territory is.

## Profile 1 — "The Warm Companion"

**The idea:** Kin is the warm, thoughtful friend who remembers everyone for you. It feels
like a personal journal kept by someone who cares — handcrafted, human, a little analog.

- **Name treatment / wordmark:** "Kin" set in a soft humanist or lightly hand-drawn wordmark;
  lowercase, generous warmth. The dot of an *i* or a small heart/thread motif as the mark.
  Feels written *to you*, not branded *at you*.
- **Personality:** Warm, attentive, sincere, unhurried. The friend who asks "how did the
  interview go?" because they actually remembered.
- **Voice & tone:** Second-person, conversational, gently encouraging. Uses the person's name.
  Warm but never saccharine; never guilt. "It's been a while" not "You've neglected them."
- **Visual feel:**
  - *Color:* warm neutrals — cream, oat, terracotta, a soft sage. Cozy, like a lamplit room.
  - *Type:* humanist sans for UI; an occasional handwritten/serif accent for warmth.
  - *Icon/illustration:* soft hand-drawn line illustrations of people, mugs, letters, threads.
  - *Health rings:* framed as **warmth** — "glowing" (in touch) → "cooling" (drifting). A warm
    amber ring that gently fades rather than a clinical red alert.
- **Positioning:** *For people who want to be a better friend — Kin is the warm companion that
  remembers what matters, so no one you love slips quietly away.*
- **Touchpoints (in voice):**
  - **Daily nudge:** *"Morning, Sven ☕ Three people are on your mind today — Mara, Dad, and Leo."*
  - **Deck cleared:** *"That's everyone for today. The people you love are a little closer now. 💛"*
  - **Weekly recap:** *"This week you reconnected with 8 people. Mara mentioned her new job — you'll remember that next time."*

## Profile 2 — "The Calm Keeper"

**The idea:** Kin is a quiet, premium, almost invisible tool for the intentional person.
It respects your attention and your privacy. Calm technology: it does its job and gets out
of the way. The opposite of a noisy social app.

- **Name treatment / wordmark:** "Kin" in a refined, low-contrast grotesk or an elegant serif;
  lots of letter-spacing and air. Monochrome. A single hairline mark, if any. Editorial,
  Kinfolk-magazine restraint.
- **Personality:** Calm, considered, discreet, grown-up. Quietly confident; never shouts.
- **Voice & tone:** Spare and plain. Short sentences. No exclamation marks, no emoji, no hype.
  Trustworthy and adult. "5 people due today." It assumes you're capable; it just keeps the thread.
- **Visual feel:**
  - *Color:* near-monochrome — paper white, ink, one muted accent (slate or muted clay).
  - *Type:* elegant editorial pairing; generous whitespace; small, calm typography.
  - *Icon/illustration:* minimal line marks, almost no illustration; negative space does the work.
  - *Health rings:* a thin, understated arc — present but quiet. Status by subtle weight/opacity,
    not loud red/green. Information, not alarm.
- **Positioning:** *For thoughtful people who guard their attention — Kin is the quiet keeper of
  your relationships, private by design and calm by default.*
- **Touchpoints (in voice):**
  - **Daily nudge:** *"5 people to reach today."*
  - **Deck cleared:** *"You're all caught up."*
  - **Weekly recap:** *"This week: 8 people, 11 conversations. Everything is on your device."*

## Profile 3 — "The Gentle Coach"

**The idea:** Kin makes staying in touch feel good — a small, satisfying daily win. It leans
into "delight as a game," but *gently*: encouragement and streaks, never pressure or shame.
The energy of a supportive coach, not a drill sergeant.

- **Name treatment / wordmark:** "Kin" in a friendly, rounded, bolder geometric; bright and
  optimistic. Room for a small, warm character/mascot or an expressive thread-knot mark that
  can animate (celebrate on a deck-clear).
- **Personality:** Upbeat, encouraging, a touch playful. Celebrates effort. Has a smile.
- **Voice & tone:** Friendly and motivating, light humor, tasteful emoji. Celebratory on wins,
  *forgiving* on misses — "welcome back," never "you broke your streak." Reframes the chore as a win.
- **Visual feel:**
  - *Color:* brighter, cheerful palette — a confident primary (coral/blue), playful secondaries.
  - *Type:* rounded geometric sans, friendly and bold.
  - *Icon/illustration:* expressive, characterful illustrations; motion, confetti (already in the
    app on deck-clear), satisfying micro-animations.
  - *Health rings:* a "connection meter" you keep topped up — playful progress rings, gentle
    pulse when due. Green = thriving; the overdue state nudges, it doesn't scold.
- **Positioning:** *For people who want a friendlier social life with less guilt — Kin turns
  staying in touch into a small daily win you'll actually look forward to.*
- **Touchpoints (in voice):**
  - **Daily nudge:** *"Ready for today's 3? 🎯 A few good people are waiting to hear from you."*
  - **Deck cleared:** *"Deck cleared! 🎉 12-day streak of being a great friend. See you tomorrow."*
  - **Weekly recap:** *"What a week — 8 people, 11 chats! 🌟 You're on a roll. (And hey, if next week's quieter, that's okay too.)"*

## Profile 4 — "The Living Garden"

**The idea:** Relationships are a garden you tend. Kin is the gentle, organic metaphor system
where connections grow, bloom, or quietly wilt — and a little regular care keeps everything
alive. Nurturing, natural, and emotionally resonant; the metaphor maps perfectly onto cadence
and the existing health rings.

- **Name treatment / wordmark:** "Kin" with an organic, growing feel — a soft botanical accent
  (a leaf, sprig, or blooming dot). Natural, hand-touched but tidy. The mark can "grow."
- **Personality:** Nurturing, patient, gently wise. The calm gardener who knows nothing blooms
  overnight and nothing thrives untended.
- **Voice & tone:** Warm and metaphor-rich but light-handed — "tend," "bloom," "drifting,"
  "a little water." Soothing, never preachy. Forgiveness baked into the metaphor: a wilting
  plant just needs attention, it isn't a failure.
- **Visual feel:**
  - *Color:* botanical — sage, moss, bloom-pink, soft earth and sky. Natural and alive.
  - *Type:* soft humanist sans with organic curves; airy.
  - *Icon/illustration:* leaves, sprigs, blooms; growth states; seasonal motifs.
  - *Health rings:* **the strongest fit** — the ring *is* the plant's health. Green = thriving/in
    bloom, yellow = thirsty/needs water, red = wilting (recoverable with a little care). Reframes
    "overdue" from a guilt-red alarm into "this one could use some water." Confetti → petals/bloom
    on a deck-clear.
- **Positioning:** *For people who want their relationships to flourish — Kin helps you tend the
  people you care about, a little each day, so the bonds that matter keep blooming.*
- **Touchpoints (in voice):**
  - **Daily nudge:** *"🌱 A few of your people could use a little water today — Mara, Dad, and Leo."*
  - **Deck cleared:** *"Your garden's tended for today. Everything's a little greener. 🌿"*
  - **Weekly recap:** *"This week you tended 8 relationships. Mara's is back in bloom. 🌸"*

## Notes for the persona test (kin-5ec.2)

Things to probe when scoring these against the personas:

- **The guilt line.** Every direction must clear the bar set in the brief: gentle, forgiving,
  never shaming. Watch where a persona reads *any* profile as naggy or judgmental.
- **The age split.** Hypothesis: Coach (3) skews young, Calm Keeper (2) skews older/professional.
  The test should confirm or break this.
- **The privacy buyer.** Only Calm Keeper (2) foregrounds local-first explicitly. If privacy is a
  strong purchase driver for several personas, that lever may need grafting onto the winner.
- **Cultural read across regions** (AU / EU / US / Latam / Dubai / Singapore) — warmth, emoji,
  and nature metaphors may land differently across cultures; note where a profile travels well or poorly.
- **Hybrids are allowed.** The output of kin-5ec.2 can recommend a winner *plus* a graft (e.g.
  "Living Garden's metaphor with Calm Keeper's privacy backbone").
